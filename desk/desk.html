    Adoption ladder

      What this dashboard cannot tell you

      • The numbers are self-reported by the install's page. They are not authenticated, and the counter cannot prove an event was real.
      • There is no time of day. Nothing shows when in the day a warning fired.
      • The counter cannot tell people apart. One person may count twice, and two people may count as one.
      • Cells under 10 are hidden, and one more cell is hidden so a hidden value cannot be worked out from a total.
      • Rates are help clicks over exposures. A click is not proof that someone got help, and leaving a page is not proof of anything.
