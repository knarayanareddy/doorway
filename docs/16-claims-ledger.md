# Claims ledger

Every quantitative or factual claim in `README.md`, `INSTALL.md`, `docs/01`, `docs/03`, `docs/04` and `docs/05` that a juror could check, with its source and its status.

**Status key**

- **sourced**: the figure or fact is in a file in this repo (`research/`, `organiser/` or the code), or at a URL named here. The quote or location is given. Where the text now differs from the source, the row says so.
- **unsourced-hypothesis**: no source in the repo. The text now labels it as a target, estimate, plan or founder's view, or it was reworded to a hypothesis.
- **removed**: taken out of the text, because it was unsourced, wrong or stale.

Sources: `research/unicri-research.html` (the dossier, "the dossier"), `research/03-dataset-ping-app.html`, `organiser/unicri/BRIEF.md`, `organiser/unicri/research/*.md`, and the arXiv abstract for Ó Ciardha et al. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06417, read 7 November 2026).
