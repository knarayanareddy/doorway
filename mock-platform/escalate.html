Keep pressing and watch it escalate

Each press is a trigger on this forum. The Door answers with a different shape each time, in this order: gate, fork, wave, hallway, door. Fork and Wave are unreviewed labs, shown here on mock data.

After each Door, this page moves on by itself, as a visitor who walks away would. The Door counts leaving as not responding, which is how it escalates. If you close the Door yourself, that counts as a response.
